# 社区谱面许可 / Fan-made chart licences

仓库根目录的 MIT 许可证适用于程序代码。社区投稿按每个目录的 `info.json` 与 `CREDITS.md` 标注许可，不能因仓库使用 MIT 就推断其中的音频或封面也使用 MIT。

提交者按 [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) 将其有权贡献的谱面数据以 [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) 提供。`chart_license` 可以标注 CC0-1.0、CC-BY-4.0、MIT、0BSD 或 Unlicense 等更宽松的额外许可；投稿仍包含 CC BY-SA 4.0 的授权。改编作品必须满足原作的适用许可及许可兼容要求。

音频和封面保留各自明确标注的许可。当前投稿允许的标识为 CC0-1.0、CC-BY-4.0、CC-BY-SA-4.0、CC-BY-NC-4.0、CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 或 LicenseRef-Permission（明确书面许可）。最后一种必须链接允许本免费游戏再分发该资源的书面授权。非商业许可资源只能用于符合其非商业条款的发行，不能由此声称可商业再利用。维护者逐项审核许可、署名和证据后决定是否收录。

模板中的谱面骨架可按 CC0-1.0 使用：<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>。模板没有音频或封面授权。

## English

The root MIT licence applies to program code. Each submitted folder's `info.json` and `CREDITS.md` specify the chart, audio and optional cover licences separately. Repository hosting does not relicense third-party media under MIT.

Submission grants CC BY-SA 4.0 for the contributed chart data. An allowed more permissive `chart_license` may offer an additional grant; derivative works must also satisfy their original licences. Audio and covers retain their stated licences and required attribution. Accepted media IDs are listed above. LicenseRef-Permission requires linked written permission for redistribution in this free game. NC assets retain their noncommercial restrictions; inclusion does not grant commercial reuse. Maintainers review evidence and compatibility individually. Automated validation does not establish legal rights.

The template chart skeleton is available under CC0-1.0; no media rights are asserted by the template.
